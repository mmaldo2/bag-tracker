Where to Hunt and how each place tells you something new showed up

Gem

One search across eBay, Poshmark, Depop, Etsy, Mercari, The RealReal and hundreds of small vintage shops. The best first stop for anything on this list.

Make a free account, search a bag, then save the search and turn on alerts.

gem.app

eBay

The deepest supply of Y2K Coach by far, and the only place with reliable sold-price history.

Save a search sorted by "newly listed"; eBay emails once a day when new matches appear. Always check "sold items" before paying asking price.

ebay.com

Mercari

Cheaper than eBay for common bags, and sellers accept offers freely.

Save a search and turn on notifications under Settings; pushes arrive with a delay, email arrives as a daily digest.

mercari.com

Poshmark

Lots of Coach, priced high but everyone haggles. Bundle two items from one closet for a discount.

Tap the star to save a search. New matches only show as a red dot in your Saved tab, so open the app every day or two.

poshmark.com

Depop

Where the "cherry charm on a Soho flap" crowd actually sells. Styled photos, quick turnover.

Star a search to save it and opt in to notifications; Depop sends one weekly push per saved search, and saved searches can't be edited, only remade.

depop.com

Etsy

Curated vintage sellers with good photos and honest condition notes. Slightly pricier, far fewer fakes.

Favorite the shops that keep turning up; they restock in batches.

etsy.com

Beni

A free app and browser extension that searches 40+ resale sites at once.

Set an alert for a bag and it emails you when a match appears. A good backup to Gem.

joinbeni.com

Search by style number wherever you can ("Coach 1463"), then by the name sellers actually use ("Coach Soho flap black"), then by the loose description ("Coach signature flap silver buckle"). Rare bags are usually listed by people who don't know what they have.

Before You Buy a two-minute check that saves the disappointment

Ask the seller for

  • A photo of the creed patch inside (the leather stamp with "No." and a code like F05K-1463). The last four digits are the style number.
  • The bottom corners. Jacquard wears through there first and it can't be fixed.
  • The inside lining under a light. Pen marks and makeup stains hide in the shadows.
  • Hardware close-ups. Silver plating flakes on the buckles and rings and shows brass underneath.

Signs it's real

  • The Cs on the front are mirrored in pairs and sit centered and symmetrical, not sliced off at the seams.
  • Stitching is even and tight, especially where the leather trim meets fabric.
  • The hangtag is a plain leather lozenge with "COACH" stamped, on a chain or ring.
  • Under-$40 "new with tags" is a warning sign. These were $200 to $400 bags new.

A fair price: check eBay's sold listings for the same style, then aim for the middle. Ask about musty smell before buying; jacquard holds onto it. A little handle creasing and light corner scuffing are normal for twenty-year-old bags and are fine to pay less for.

If You Only Do Three Things
  • Make a Gem account and save one search per bag with alerts on. That covers most of the internet at once.
  • Save the two rare ones (the leopard patchwork tote and the pink Poppy) on eBay, Mercari and Depop too. Those are the ones that vanish in an hour.
  • Before paying, look at eBay's sold listings for that style. Asking prices on Poshmark are a wish, not a price.
